The parsing of semantic structure of research articles can be widely used to address multiple issues such as information extraction and retrieval. This paper describes the semantic structure of research articles by applying machine learning techniques to recognize the semantic types of discourse segments in these articles. We extracted macro structure of research articles, syntactic and lexical information of each discourse segment as input features, and trained five models, namely support vector machines (SVM), conditional random fields (CRF), random forests (RF), gradient boost classifier (GBC), and stochastic gradient descent classifier (SGD). We integrated three best-performing models, i.e., CRF, SVM, and GBC, to form a bagging model for classifying all discourse segments from the full text. Experiments showed that our bagging model outperformed the baseline model on tasks of classifying discourse segments from full text and result sections with a higher accuracy and F-score. Additionally, this paper implemented a topic-clustering experiment to show the effectiveness of the model on topic detection, a common task in the field of text mining. 
Key words: research article; semantic labeling; text classification; machine learning; clustering
